# Corporation Mechanics

## Overview
Corporations are player-created groups that enable teamwork, resource pooling, and shared progression.

## Creation
- **Cost**: $500,000 in-game cash (deducted from founder).
- **Founder**: Automatically assigned the “CEO” role.
- **Name & Tag**: Unique 4‑letter tag (e.g., [MEGA]), full name up to 30 chars.
- **Member cap**: Starts at 5, can be upgraded via corp bank.
- Visible in a public directory; invite‑only or open recruitment toggle.

## Roles & Permissions
| Role | Permissions |
|------|-------------|
| **CEO** | Full control: invite/kick, change roles, withdraw/deposit bank, dissolve corp, upgrade corp perks. |
| **CFO** | Manage corp bank (deposit/withdraw with limits), view financial logs. |
| **Officer** | Invite members, moderate chat, start corp votes. |
| **Member** | Chat, view corp status, contribute to bank, benefit from passive corp perks. |

## Corporation Bank
- Shared pool of cash visible to all members.
- **Deposits**: Any member can deposit cash; logs recorded.
- **Withdrawals**: CEO or CFO (with daily limit). Large withdrawals (>25% of bank) trigger a 24h vote among officers.
- Bank earns **interest** (0.5% per day) to incentivise saving.
- Can be used to buy **Corporate Assets** (stocks, businesses, properties) which generate revenue shared among members.

## Profit Sharing
- **Passive Income Split**: Corp‑owned businesses/real estate generate revenue deposited into bank every hour. Members receive dividends (automatic deposit) based on their **contribution share**.
- **Contribution Share** = (Player’s total deposited cash) / (Total corp deposits), recalculated weekly.
- Dividends are tax‑free (no fee) to encourage participation.

## Corporation Perks (Upgradeable with Corp Bank)
- Increased member cap (up to 50).
- Reduced marketplace fees (3% instead of 5%).
- Exclusive corp cosmetics (logo, chat badge).
- Corp‑wide production boost for manufacturing (5% per level).

## Dissolution
- CEO can dissolve if bank < $100k and vote >66% passes.
- On dissolve, all corporate assets liquidated at 80% market value, cash distributed to members by contribution share.
- All members returned to “Independent” status.

## Chat & Communication
- Dedicated corp chat channel (visible only to members).
- Pinned announcements by CEO/Officers.
- Integration with global chat optional.

## Multi‑Corp Limits
- A player can belong to only **one** corporation at a time.
- Leaving a corp has a 24‑hour cooldown before joining another.
- If kicked, cooldown is 48 hours (except if corp dissolved).
