# Reputation System Rules

## Overview
A numeric score (‑100 to +100) representing a player’s ethical standing in the game world.  
Reputation influences NPC deals, player trust, and unlockable perks.

## Base Scale
- **‑100 to ‑31**: “Shady” – access to black‑market deals, but high‑end contracts blocked.
- **‑30 to +30**: “Neutral” – standard deals.
- **+31 to +70**: “Trusted” – premium loans, preferred marketplace visibility.
- **+71 to +100**: “Paragon” – exclusive charity events, 5% global income boost.

## Actions That Affect Reputation
| Action | Rep Change |
|--------|------------|
| **Fair Wages** (upgrade staff morale) | +2 per level |
| **Charity Donation** (donate cash in‑game) | +1 per $10,000 |
| **Honor Contract** (complete a player‑to‑player contract on time) | +5 |
| **Help Startup** (fund a startup fully) | +3 |
| **Lay off Staff** (reduce staff level) | ‑3 per level |
| **Hostile Takeover** (successful) | ‑15 |
| **Break Contract** (fail to deliver) | ‑10 |
| **Market Manipulation** (buy/sell your own listing via alt, detected) | ‑20 (plus admin action) |

## Cooldowns & Decay
- Reputation naturally drifts toward 0 at a rate of ±1 per week (e.g., +80 → +79).
- Repeated actions within 24h have diminishing returns (first charity = +3, second = +2, etc.)

## Impact on Gameplay
- **Loan Interest Rate**: Trusted/Paragon get 2% lower interest from NPC bank.
- **Marketplace Trust Badge**: High reputation sellers get a “Verified” icon, boosting sales.
- **Corp Invites**: CEOs see reputation when considering applicants.
- **NPC Contracts**: Some high‑value supplier deals require rep ≥ 40.
- **Audit Risk**: Shady players have a 5% daily chance of “audit” (lose 10% of cash) – incentive to stay neutral.

## Visibility
- Other players see your reputation badge (e.g., “Trusted Trader”) but not exact score.
- Hover/tap on badge shows vague description.
- Score visible only to player on Profile screen.
